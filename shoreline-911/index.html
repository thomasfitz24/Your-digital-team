About Shoreline

Classic Porsche Lines, Reimagined With Modern Performance

Shoreline builds custom Porsche 911s that look like they rolled out of the 1970s but drive like a stripped back, reliable, modernized classic. Every build starts with a hand-picked donor car and is rebuilt from the ground up, with a custom interior, upgraded engine, and tuned suspension. No two are alike. Built the way you want it.

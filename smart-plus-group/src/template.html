Electrical Solutions We Offer

Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Points

Tired of queuing at public charging stations to power your vehicle? Our EV charging point installation services make it simple to plug in and charge from the comfort and convenience of your driveway.

EICR – Electrical Installation Condition Report

We help landlords protect their property investments and keep tenants safe by providing thorough Electrical Installation Condition Reports. Our qualified electricians identify any defects, wear, damage, or potential hazards.

Consumer Unit Update (New Fuse Board)

Is your fuse board outdated or in need of an upgrade? We specialise in installing modern and safer fuse boards that fully comply with the latest electrical regulations.

Partial/Full Rewire

If your home's wiring is outdated, damaged or potentially unsafe, a rewire may be necessary. Our experienced electricians handle all aspects of the rewiring process, from planning to certification.

Electrical Breakdown Callouts

Have you unexpectedly lost power? Our team provides fast and reliable breakdown services to restore power and address any electrical problems you may encounter, swiftly and professionally.

Home Renovation Electrical Installation

Planning a home renovation or extension? Our electricians have the expertise to handle all your electrical installation needs. From lighting fixtures to power outlets and appliances.

Exterior Power and Lighting

Enhance the security, safety and aesthetics of your outdoor space. Whether you need outdoor sockets, security lighting or decorative lighting to create ambience, we have you covered.

Meet the Sweep Sisters

Adele and Sian started Sweep Sisters because a thorough sweep and a tidy house should not be a trade-off. We are a female-led team, HETAS certified and fully insured, and we work the way we would want someone working in our own homes.

We sweep everything from Victorian terraces in Chelmsford and Colchester to modern stoves in Brentwood and Georgian townhouses in Hertford. The team you speak to on the phone is the team that turns up.

Every visit runs the same way. Dust sheets down, vacuum on, a full sweep, a smoke test, and a HETAS certificate for your insurer. If we spot a problem you get photos and a straight answer about whether it needs doing now or can wait. No pressure either way.

Common questions

Who are Sweep Sisters?

A female-led, HETAS certified and fully insured chimney sweeping business working across Essex and Hertfordshire. Read more about us.

Which areas do you cover?

Most of Essex and Hertfordshire, including Chelmsford, Colchester, Brentwood, Hertford, St Albans and Watford. See our Essex and Hertfordshire pages, or send your postcode and we'll confirm.

How much does a chimney sweep cost?

£95 for a standard sweep, including a visual inspection, smoke test, clean-up and certificate. A CCTV inspection adds £75, or it's £170 for both. See all chimney services and prices.

Do I need a sweep or a full inspection?

If your fire or stove is in regular, trouble-free use, a standard sweep is usually enough. If you've just moved in, haven't used the chimney for a while, or have noticed smoke, smells or poor draw, a sweep plus CCTV inspection is the safer place to start.

Can you come out urgently?

Same-day appointments are often available, and we offer 24/7 emergency cover. If you think your chimney is on fire, call 999 first. Otherwise, call 0330 133 2121.

    The three questions we get most

    How do I book?

    Pick a slot on the timetable or go straight to the booking page. All visits must be booked before you arrive; we don't accept walk-ins.

    See this week's timetable

    Where can I park?

    The Yard offers limited free parking. If the car park is full, street parking should be available in the surrounding area.

    How many adults can come?

    Up to two adults per child for open and pre-walking sessions. Tickets are per child, so two children need two tickets.

    Getting to The Yard

    Around 30 minutes from Newcastle and 20 minutes from the MetroCentre. Our premises are in The Yard, the former Masonic Hall building. You will see roadside signage for both Wonder Sensory and Wonder Café.

Come and enjoy delicious food, homemade cakes and freshly baked scones, alongside a wide range of drinks, from barista-style coffee to matcha and more. Homemade cakes & scones

We bake fresh, homemade cakes in-house, using quality ingredients to create slices that are full of flavour. From rich chocolate cakes to fruity favourites, there's something for every sweet craving. We also serve freshly baked scones, soft and golden, perfect with a little butter, jam, or cream.

Our cakes and scones are a perfect match for our barista-style coffee, carefully made to your taste, or a comforting cup of classic English breakfast tea, decaf, or a selection of herbal infusions.

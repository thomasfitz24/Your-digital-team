A gym that starts where you are

StrongHer is a women-only studio in Washington. It exists because so many women have stood outside a gym and quietly decided it wasn't for them. Too loud, too busy, too many people who already look like they know what they're doing.

Inside, it's small and it's calm. You're shown how everything works before you're left to get on with it, and every class has options so you can take it at the level you're at that day. Trained for years or never set foot in a gym, there's a place here for you.

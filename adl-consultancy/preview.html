How certification works

Four stages, and we're with you for all of them

No stage is handed back to you as homework. We write the system, run the internal audit and sit in the room on assessment day.

STAGE 01

Gap analysis

We review what you already do against the standard and tell you honestly how far away you are — and what it will cost.

STAGE 02

Build the system

Procedures written around your real workflow, complex text replaced with clear flowcharts your team will actually follow.

STAGE 03

Internal audit

We audit you before the assessor does, so nothing in the external visit is a surprise to anyone.

STAGE 04

Assessment & beyond

We attend your BSI, NQA or LRQA visit, then keep the system alive through surveillance and recertification.

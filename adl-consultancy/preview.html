01 — The problem

Most ISO projects stall in the same three places

Businesses rarely fail certification because the standard is beyond them. They stall because the work lands on someone who already has a full-time job.

01

The manual nobody reads

A consultant hands over a 200-page template written for a business that isn't yours. It sits in a drawer, the auditor finds the gap between the document and reality, and you fail on the first surveillance visit.

02

It becomes a second job

The work falls to an ops manager or an office manager on top of their real role. Progress happens in gaps between other work, deadlines slip, and the tender you needed it for closes.

03

Nobody is there on the day

The assessor arrives and asks questions no one has been prepared for. Findings are raised that a rehearsal would have caught, and the certificate is delayed by another cycle.

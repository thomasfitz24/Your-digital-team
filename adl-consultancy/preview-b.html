Questions we get asked most

If yours isn't here, call us on 01279 293007 and we'll answer it straight.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. We operate with a flexible approach — there is no contract to sign and you are free to exit the process if your circumstances change.

How long does certification take?

It depends on the standard, your size and how much you already have documented. We tell you honestly at the gap analysis stage, before you commit to anything.

Will you be there for the external audit?

Yes. We provide support during external visits from BSI, NQA, LRQA and the other registration bodies. You are never left to face the assessor alone.

Can you handle more than one standard at once?

Yes, and it is usually cheaper and simpler. We integrate multiple standards into a single management system so you run one set of processes and one audit cycle.

Do you work outside Essex and London?

Our consultants are based in Essex and work throughout London and the surrounding counties. We schedule visits at times that suit you.

What if we already have a system in place?

That is fine — we update and streamline existing systems as often as we build new ones, replacing complex textual procedures with simple, colourful flowcharts.

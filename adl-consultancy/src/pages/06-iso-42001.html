What is ISO 42001?

AI is evolving fast. If your business is predominantly using or selling AI services, it's important to be aware of the ISO AI standards.

ISO 42001, also known as ISO/IEC 42001, is the latest global standard for using AI in businesses by the ISO and IEC to ensure AI systems are being managed responsibly.

By being ISO 42001 registered, it assures your customers that your AI services are safe, ethical, and compliant, and ensures you are up to date with any future legal changes. With very few businesses in the UK having ISO/IEC 42001 certification, achieving it early gives you a competitive advantage, instilling confidence in your customers and stakeholders.

Inside the standard

What an AI management system covers

The ISO AI standards focus on the following.

  • Risk management and impact — identify risks associated with AI systems throughout their lifecycle and evaluate the impacts of using AI, helping mitigate negative effects
  • Security and safety — ensuring AI systems are protected from cyber attacks and unauthorised access, and keep them operating reliably
  • Ethical AI development — fairness and transparency in AI decisions, and accountability for outcomes. Data quality must be accurate to train AI systems and avoid errors and biases
  • Performance optimisation — processes for monitoring AI systems and enhancing them in future, ensuring efficient operation and legal compliance

Who needs the ISO AI standards?

If your business is selling AI services, it will need the ISO 42001 certificate. No matter if you are a start-up or a large enterprise, this standard is designed to be used for the development and management of AI systems. Industries in tech, finance and healthcare need to prove their AI is ethical, unbiased and safe — even if a company is using AI tools like chatbots. The more AI in your business directly impacts the customer, the more important it is that you meet these standards.

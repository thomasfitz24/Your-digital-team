What is ISO 42001?

ISO 42001, also known as ISO/IEC 42001, is the latest global standard for using AI in businesses by the ISO and IEC to ensure AI systems are being managed responsibly. The ISO AI standards focus on the following:

Risk management and impact

Identify risks associated with AI systems throughout their lifecycle and evaluate the impacts of using AI, helping mitigate negative effects.

Security and safety

Ensuring AI systems are protected from cyber attacks and unauthorised access, and keep them operating reliably.

Ethical AI development

Meaning fairness and transparency in AI decisions and accountability for the outcomes of their AI systems. Data quality must be accurate to train AI systems to avoid errors and biases.

Performance optimisation

Have processes for monitoring AI systems and enhancing them in the future, ensuring efficient operation and compliance legally.

Who needs ISO 42001?

If your business is selling AI services, it will need the ISO 42001 certificate. No matter if you are a start-up or a large enterprise, this standard is designed to be used for the development and management of AI systems.

Some well-known industries in tech, finance, and healthcare need to prove their AI is ethical, unbiased, and safe, even if a company is using AI tools like chatbots. The more AI in your business directly impacts the customer, the more important it is that you meet these standards. Using an ISO framework makes sure your business is compliant with the use of AI now and in the future, giving you peace of mind.

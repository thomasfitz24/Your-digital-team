QEC UK offers certification and training to ISO Standards. QEC UK is the UK agency of QEC Certification which forms part of SN Registrars (Holdings) Ltd, the UKAS Accredited certification body. Forming part of a global certification spanning over 40 countries holding over 14,000 certificates worldwide. You can find SN Registrars (Holdings) Ltd under the UKAS listing 8327 operating under the trading name of QEC Certification.

First Responders

First aid training

They offer courses to industry, commerce and the community. Founded in 1989, they were one of the first independent training companies. They offer regulated RQF qualifications by Qualsafe Awards and their own three year certification — Qualsafe Awards centre no. 904798, with all courses Health & Safety compliant.

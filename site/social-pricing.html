●03 · The honest comparison

What each post actually costs.

Most agencies won't show you this, because it makes one of their plans look bad. Here it is anyway: divide the monthly price by the number of posts and you get what you're paying per piece.

Growth is the cheapest per post at £9.17, and Multi-Platform is the dearest at £16.25.

Per-post cost is a useful sanity check and a terrible way to choose. Pick the plan that matches the number of platforms you actually need to be good on, then look at the volume.

●04 · The add-on

Someone answers the messages.

Posting is only half of it. The comment asking what something costs is a lead, and left for three days it's a lead somebody else got. Inbox management covers replying to comments and DMs, flagging genuine enquiries to you, and making sure nothing is missed.

Starter & Growth
+£40/month

One inbox to watch. Comments and DMs answered, genuine enquiries flagged straight to you.

Multi-Platform
+£80/month

Three inboxes, three sets of notifications and three sets of conventions — monitored across all of them.

Full Presence
+£100/month

Full daily monitoring across all three platforms, with priority on anything that reads like a buying question.

It scales with platforms, not with posts — three platforms means three inboxes to watch, each with its own notifications and its own conventions. That's why it costs more on the multi-platform plans than on the single-platform ones.

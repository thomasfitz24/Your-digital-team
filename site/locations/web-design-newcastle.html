How much does web design cost in Newcastle?

From £150 for a single page, £500 for a basic two-to-four page site, £1,200 for a small business site with SEO included, and £2,000 for e-commerce. Those are one-off build prices. You get one number in writing before anything starts.

Are you actually in Newcastle?

We are based in Bishop Auckland, County Durham, and work across Tyneside every week. If a meeting helps we come to you, whether that is the Quayside, Jesmond, Gosforth or one of the business parks off the A1. Most builds run over email and a shared link and never need one.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. Every build is handed over with a walkthrough. Text, images, prices and opening hours are all yours to change without calling anyone.

What happens to my rankings when the site changes?

We keep every URL that already ranks, or map it to its new home with a redirect. Rankings drop when links get lost, not when a site gets better, and we do not lose them.

Is SEO included?

On the Small business and E-commerce tiers, yes: structure, page titles, local schema and your Google Business Profile matched to the site. One-page and Basic builds get the fundamentals, and ongoing SEO is available monthly if you want to push further.

How long does a build take?

A one-page site in about a week, a basic site in about two, a small business site in about four and e-commerce in about six. The clock starts when we have your words and photos.

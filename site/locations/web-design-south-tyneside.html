01Where we work

Serving South Shields, North Shields & Tynemouth

South Shields, North Shields and Tynemouth share a river mouth, a ferry and a lot of customers, and they are still three different places with three different searches. Someone looking for a cafe on Ocean Road types South Shields. Someone after fish on the Fish Quay types North Shields. Someone booking a table on Front Street types Tynemouth. One thin page per town would be the lazy answer and Google would filter it. One page that actually knows the three towns is the honest one.

That is what this is. We build for the businesses along the coast at Sandhaven and Longsands, the trades working out of Jarrow, Hebburn and the Boldons, the guesthouses and bars around the Priory, and the shops and services that both sides of the river already know by name. The site names your town, matches your Google profile to it, and puts the call one tap away.

Most of these businesses do not need a big build. The one-page site at £150 exists for exactly this: a business that is already trusted locally and just needs to be found by the person who was recommended it last night.

FAQs

01I only have a Facebook page. Is a website worth it?

If people search your name and Facebook comes up, you are losing the ones who do not use it, and Google shows Facebook pages less reliably than a real site. A one-page site at £150 fixes that and links to your Facebook rather than replacing it.

02Do you cover North Shields and Tynemouth as well as South Shields?

Yes. They are across the river and in a different borough, but they are the same coast and the same customers. The site names whichever town you are in, matched exactly to your Google Business Profile.

03How quickly can it be live?

A one-page site in about a week from the day we have your words and photos. Basic sites in about two weeks.

04Can I change my opening hours myself?

Yes, and your Google Business Profile at the same time. We show you both in the handover so a Sunday change takes two minutes.

05What do I need to give you?

Your words, or a rough version we tidy up, and your own photos. If you have neither, we can work from a phone call and a visit. Bring a logo if you have one; if not, we set your name in the right type.

06Do you need to meet me?

No. Most builds run over email and a shared link you check on your own phone. If a meeting helps, we will come to you.

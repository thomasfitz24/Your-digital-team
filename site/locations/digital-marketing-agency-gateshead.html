One plan

Full-service digital marketing for Gateshead

Gateshead is really two economies. There is the business-to-business world of Team Valley and the estates along the A1, where a customer researches for weeks, reads three suppliers’ sites and then picks up the phone. And there is the consumer world of the Quays, the MetroCentre, Low Fell and Whickham, where someone searches on a Saturday morning and books the first business that answers.

Those need different marketing. The first wants a strong search presence, considered content and remarketing that keeps you in view for the weeks a decision takes. The second wants the map pack, fast ads on the searches that convert, and reviews that update. Most agencies sell one playbook to both. We build the plan from which market you are actually in, and we serve both sides of the river without pretending Gateshead is a suburb of Newcastle. Businesses here search “Gateshead”, and so does Google.

Whatever the mix, you get the same thing at the end of each month: one figure for what it cost per enquiry, and a plain-English note on what we are changing next.

Questions

FAQs

Do I have to take every channel?

No. The consultation says which channels are worth running for your market and in what order. A Team Valley supplier and a Low Fell salon get different plans, and neither gets a channel that will not pay.

How is ad spend handled?

You pay Google directly for the clicks. Our fee covers managing the account, building the landing pages and tracking every conversion. Spend and results arrive in the same weekly note.

How do you measure results?

Calls, forms and emails tracked to the channel that produced them, rolled up to one figure a month: cost per enquiry. Rankings and traffic are reported too, but they are not the number that matters.

What if my website is not good enough?

The consultation checks. If the site will waste the budget, we quote a rebuild first and say plainly why. We do not run ads into a page that will not convert.

Do you work in Newcastle as well?

Yes. Most of our Gateshead clients sell across the river too. We plan for the one market customers see and the two markets Google sees, and we have a dedicated Newcastle web design page if that is where you are.

Is there a contract?

Monthly, with a month’s notice. Everything built, written and tracked stays yours.

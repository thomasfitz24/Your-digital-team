FAQs

I am a sole trader. Do I really need more than one page?

Usually not. A one-page site at £150 with your services, areas, reviews and a tap-to-call number does the job for most trades. If you later want a page per service so each ranks on its own, we add them.

Can you make the reviews show automatically?

Yes. Your Google rating and count are pulled onto the page and stay current, so you are not copying and pasting reviews every month.

How fast can it be live?

A one-page site in about a week from the day we have your words and photos. A basic site in about two weeks. Small business builds around four.

What if my photos are not great?

We will tell you. Phone photos in daylight of finished work usually beat stock images. If you need a photographer we can point you to one, but most trades sites do fine without.

Do you cover Washington and Houghton?

Yes, and Hetton, Seaham and the coast. Where you work goes on the page and on your Google Business Profile. Most builds run over email and a shared link, so distance only matters for what we write on the site.

Do I own the site?

Yes. Domain in your name, site on a platform you can log into, and a walkthrough so you can change prices, hours and photos yourself.

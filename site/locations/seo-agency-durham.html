01Local SEO

Why local SEO matters for Durham businesses

County Durham is not one place. It is a cathedral city, a dozen market towns and a lot of villages between them, and the Google map pack moves every ten miles. A roofer in Chester-le-Street competes with Gateshead firms from the north. A solicitor in Durham City competes with the pull of the university. A garage in Newton Aycliffe is in a different market from one in Peterlee. City SEO is about winning one dense area. Durham SEO is about deciding which towns deserve a page of their own and which are better served by one strong regional page, then earning both.

Proximity is part of how Google decides local results, and it is part of how customers decide too. A national SEO firm with a “Durham” page has never stood on Silver Street or driven the A688 to Barnard Castle. We are in Bishop Auckland. We know which towns people mean when they say “up the county” and which searches actually lead to a phone call here.

That knowledge goes into the work: proper service pages, honest town pages only where the demand justifies them, a Google Business Profile that is actually maintained, and content that answers the questions people in County Durham type into Google at ten o’clock at night.

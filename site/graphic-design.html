●01 · What an identity is

A logo is about a fifth of it.

An identity is a set of decisions somebody else has to be able to follow: which navy, at what weight, with how much air around it, in which typeface, at what size. Get those written down and every asset made afterwards looks related. Skip them and you get a folder of files that happen to share a logo.

Switch between the three below. Each is a real identity we built, and every hex on the panel is the value actually used in the artwork further down this page — sampled from the files, not approximated.

●02 · Selected work

Prep Bite.

Meal prep & sports nutritionIdentity PackagingRetail

A meal prep brand that had to work in two places at once: on a chilled shelf, read at arm's length in about a second, and on a protein bar wrapper, where the same identity has to carry a full nutrition panel, a barcode and allergen advice without looking like a spreadsheet.

We built the mark as a P and B locked together, so it still reads at 12mm on a wrapper seal and as a submark on its own. The palette runs deep navy for the ground, a single acid green for anything that has to be found in a hurry — macros, use-by, the flavour name — and nothing else competing.

The strapline, Define Your Own Excellence, sits on every pack in the same place at the same size, which is what makes a shelf of different flavours still read as one brand.

●05 · How it runs

Four steps. No mystery.

You see work at every stage, and nothing goes to print until you have signed it off.

01

Where it's going

Before anything is drawn we establish where this has to work — a shelf, a shirt, a van, a phone screen — because a mark that only works large is a mark that fails on half of them.

02

Routes, not a reveal

You get real directions applied to real assets, not one option presented as a fait accompli. If a route is wrong you can say so while it's cheap to change.

03

Refine the chosen one

Spacing, sizes, the small-size version, the one-colour version, how it sits on photography. This is the part that decides whether it still looks right in a year.

04

Artwork and handover

Print-ready and screen-ready files, a written guide, and the source. Then you never have to come back to us just to get your own logo out of a folder.

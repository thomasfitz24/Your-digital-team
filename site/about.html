●01 · How we got here

From one frustrated founder to a working agency.

Short history, honestly told. We're young, and we'd rather say so than pretend otherwise.

  1. 2024

    The beginning

    I started Your Digital Team because I was sick of watching companies around me pay for a service and get nowhere near what they were paying for. Miscommunication, missed deadlines, or simply never delivering — real money spent, and nothing to show for it.

  2. 2025

    Starting out

    First clients, and the thing they all said was some version of the same sentence: this is what we thought we were buying last time. We're true and honest. If something isn't working we'll tell you, because truth in delivery beats no delivery — even when it's an uncomfortable call to make.

  3. Late 2025

    Katie joins

    Katie came on as co-founder, and the agency stopped being one person with strong opinions. We built something that treats your budget like our own, communicates like a partner rather than a supplier, and measures success only by what actually moved your business.

  4. 2026

    Building the company

    No disappearing acts. No vanity reports. No excuses. Nine client sites live, six services running, and a standard we hold ourselves to whether or not anyone is checking.

●02 · What we hold to

Four things we won't trade away.

Every agency has a values page. These are the four we'd actually be embarrassed to break.

01

Your results, not our portfolio

Work that looks good in a case study and does nothing for your revenue is a failure we just haven't admitted to yet. Every strategy is built for your numbers, and the reporting is honest about which parts are working.

02

We'll tell you when to stop

When a channel has hit its ceiling, when a piece of work isn't worth the budget, or when you don't need what you're about to buy — you'll hear it from us. It costs us revenue and it's the reason clients stay.

03

You talk to the people doing the work

No account manager relaying messages to a team you never meet. There are two of us and we're the ones in your ad account, so nothing gets lost between the person who promised it and the person building it.

04

You own everything

The ad accounts, the analytics, the website, the list. All of it in your name from day one. If you ever leave us you take the whole thing with you — which is exactly the mess we were built to stop happening to people.

●05 · The difference

What sets us apart.

Four things that are true of us and, in our experience, not true of most of the agencies we've replaced.

01

Small, and staying
that way

Two co-founders who do the work themselves. It caps how many clients we can take, which is a genuine limitation — and it's also why nothing gets handed to a junior you were never told about.

02

Systems,
not packages

Most agencies sell a fixed bundle because it's easier to price. We build the combination your bottleneck actually calls for, and change it when the bottleneck moves — which it always does.

03

Judged on revenue,
not activity

Impressions, clicks and rankings are inputs. We report them because they explain what's happening, but the number we're actually accountable for is the one that reaches your bank account.

04

We'll turn work
down

If we don't think we can make you meaningfully more than we cost, we'll say so at the first call and point you somewhere better. Taking the money anyway is how the cycle we're describing keeps going.

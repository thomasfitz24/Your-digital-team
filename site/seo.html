●How we work

The order we do it in.

Sequence matters. Publishing content onto a site Google can't crawl properly is money spent twice.

01

We crawl the whole site and fix what stops Google reading it: broken links, duplicate titles, orphaned pages, render-blocking scripts, slow templates. This is unglamorous and it is where most of the early movement comes from — you often have pages that deserve to rank and simply can't.

02

We map every term to a stage of your buying cycle and to what a customer from it is actually worth. A 200-a-month term where people are ready to buy beats a 20,000-a-month term where they're browsing. We'll show you the maths before we commit the roadmap.

03

Every page answers one question completely — structured so the search engine understands what it's for and the visitor has no reason to go back to the results. Titles, headings, internal links and schema all point the same way, and the cluster around it establishes that you cover the subject rather than skim it.

04

Digital PR, original data and direct outreach to publications your market already reads. No directories, no private networks, nothing from a marketplace. Bought links work right up until an update, and then they cost you more than they ever returned.

05

Rankings, traffic and revenue against the plan, reported weekly in plain English. What underperforms gets cut rather than defended. What converts gets more resource. That loop is the whole reason organic compounds instead of plateauing.

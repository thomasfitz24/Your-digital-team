How we work

No mystery. Just method.

Audit first. No long contracts. A plain-English note every week. Here is what the first three months look like, and what you hold at the end of each step.

  1. Week 1–2

    We audit everything

    Your site, your rankings, your ads, your competitors. You'll know exactly where the money is leaking and where the quickest wins are — even if you never hire us.

    You getA written audit, yours to keep
  2. Week 3

    We build the plan

    One page. Clear priorities, clear targets, clear costs. What we're doing this month, what it should produce, and how we'll measure it.

    You getA one-page plan with costs
  3. Ongoing

    We do the work

    Content shipped, campaigns live, fixes deployed. A plain-English update every week, not a quarterly deck of vanity metrics.

    You getA short note every week
  4. Month 2+

    We scale what works

    Every month we cut what underperforms and double down on what converts. Deliberate decisions, not lucky breaks.

    You getA monthly call and a revised plan

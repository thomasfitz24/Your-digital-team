Kemp Services is a commercial cleaning company based in east London. With over 30 years' experience, our team of experts can assist with all your commercial cleaning needs. Our clients are mainly based across London, Essex, Kent, Surrey, Middlesex and Hertfordshire. We also have contracts in Manchester, Newcastle, Nottingham, Liverpool, Cardiff, Birmingham.

We offer a professional, high-quality service and pride ourselves on being a flexible and responsive commercial cleaning contractor. We make a difference to the properties we clean and the clients we work with. Regardless of the type of property – office, retail, welfare facilities, warehouse – we can help keep your premises looking sparkling clean at all times.

Pre/Post Tenancy Cleans

Pre-tenancy cleaning gives the benefit of a clean and fresh start when moving into a new property. The purpose of post-tenancy cleaning is to ensure all rooms are spotless.

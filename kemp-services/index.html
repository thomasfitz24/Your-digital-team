About Kemp Services

We make a difference to the properties we clean and the clients we work with.

Our specialist London commercial cleaning contractors are ready to help you. Kemp Services is a commercial cleaning company based in east London. With over 30 years' experience, our team of experts can assist with all your commercial cleaning needs — for clients across London, Essex, Kent, Surrey, Middlesex and Hertfordshire, with contracts in Manchester, Newcastle, Nottingham, Liverpool, Cardiff and Birmingham.
